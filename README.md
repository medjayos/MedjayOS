## What is MedjayOS?

MedjayOS is a desktop-style, open-source intelligence (OSINT) workspace that runs in your browser. It brings research, note-taking, case tracking and learning material into one place, with no account and no install.

Your notes and cases are stored in your own browser, with optional passphrase encryption.

## What's inside

| App | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Operations** | Your home screen: notebook entries, active cases and the latest publications at a glance. |
| **Notebook** | A private notebook that saves as you type, with optional passphrase encryption. |
| **Case Files** | Open a case, log each finding with its source and view it on a timestamped timeline. |
| **Articles** | OSINT articles and guides from MedjayOS analysts and contributors. |
| **Field Manual** | How to get started, what each app does, keyboard shortcuts and privacy. |
| **Controls** | Lock and encrypt your data, back it up, and adjust sounds, idle lock and display mode. |
| **Quick Tools** | Small utilities for everyday investigation work. |

## Why it exists

Good investigation is not only about finding information. It is about verifying it and understanding what it shows. MedjayOS gives researchers a focused, private place to do that work.
